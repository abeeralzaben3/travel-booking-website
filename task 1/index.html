Welcome to the Capital of Scotland

Edinburgh is one of the most beautiful and historic cities in Europe. Known as the "Athens of the North", it blends medieval charm with modern elegance.

The city is home to the famous Edinburgh Castle, the Royal Mile, and the breathtaking Arthur's Seat — an ancient volcano offering panoramic views of the entire city.

Why Visit Edinburgh?

Whether you love history, literature, or festivals, Edinburgh has something for everyone. The city hosts the world-famous Edinburgh Festival Fringe every August — the largest arts festival on the planet.

Quick Facts About Edinburgh

Population: ~550,000 people
Currency: British Pound (GBP £)
Language: English & Scottish Gaelic

Pricing & Seasonal Notes

Regular ticket price: £450 £350 (limited-time offer)

Tip: Book early! Hotel prices increase by 30% during the festival season.

Fun fact: Edinburgh Castle sits on a volcanic rock formed over 340 million years ago. The city's population density is about 1,800 people/km2, and the chemical symbol for the Scottish salt is Na2CO3 — just kidding, that's soda ash not Scottish salt 😄

💡 Quick Tips for Travelers

Essential information you should know before visiting Edinburgh, Scotland.

🌤️ Weather

Edinburgh has a mild climate. Summers are cool (15–20°C), and winters are cold (0–7°C). Rain is common year-round, so always carry an umbrella ☔.

💷 Currency

The official currency is the British Pound (GBP £). Credit cards are widely accepted, but it's wise to carry some cash for small shops and pubs.

🗣️ Language

The main language is English, with Scottish Gaelic spoken in some regions. Most locals are friendly and happy to help tourists.

⏰ Time Zone

Edinburgh follows GMT (UTC+0) in winter and BST (UTC+1) in summer.

📅 Best Time to Visit

The best months to visit are May to September, when the weather is mild and the days are long. August is especially lively due to the famous Edinburgh Festival Fringe 🎭.
